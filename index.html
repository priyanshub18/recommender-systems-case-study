The Multi-Stage Retrieval Funnel

Evaluating complex deep neural networks over millions of items is computationally intractable at inference time. Production systems split the task into distinct, cascading stages of decreasing candidate volume and increasing model complexity.

Core Paradigms & Mathematical Formulation

From classical collaborative matrix factorization to modern deep dual-encoder networks and Maximum Inner Product Search (MIPS).

[FOUNDATIONAL ALGORITHM]

1. Matrix Factorization (SVD / ALS)

Projects sparse user-item interaction matrix R ∈ ℝ|U| × |I| into low-dimensional latent space d ≪ min(|U|, |I|). Affinity is predicted by inner product between user latent vector pu and item latent vector qi:

&rcirc;ui = μ + bu + bi + puT qi

Trained via Alternating Least Squares (ALS) or Stochastic Gradient Descent with L2 regularization penalty:

min ∑ (rui - &rcirc;ui)2 + λ (||pu||2 + ||qi||2 + bu2 + bi2)
[DEEP LEARNING SCALE]

2. Two-Tower Neural Retrieval

Decouples query and candidate spaces into two distinct deep networks: User Tower fθ(u) → ℝd and Item Tower gφ(i) → ℝd.

P(i | u) = exp( ⟨ fθ(u), gφ(i) ⟩ / τ ) / ∑ exp( ⟨ fθ(u), gφ(j) ⟩ / τ )

Item embeddings g(i) are pre-computed offline and indexed into sub-millisecond vector databases (HNSW, ScaNN). Online inference only executes the single forward pass of f(u) followed by a vector query.

Netflix: Two-Dimensional Page Ranking & Dynamic Artwork Bandits

Netflix's recommendation engine is not a single list ranking. It is a 2D matrix of personalized rows combined with contextual multi-armed bandits that personalize thumbnail artwork for every individual subscriber.

1. The 2D Page Generation Paradigm

The Netflix homepage solves a two-dimensional optimization problem: row selection and row ordering (vertical axis) combined with video ranking within each row (horizontal axis).

Evaluation: The Offline-to-Online Gap

High offline benchmark accuracy frequently fails in real-world production due to presentation bias, popularity bias, and feedback loops.

[OFFLINE MATHEMATICAL BENCHMARKS]

Offline Ranking Metrics

  • • NDCG@K: Evaluates logarithmic position discount of relevant items.
  • • Recall@K / HitRate@K: Measures coverage of ground truth watch events.
  • • MRR (Mean Reciprocal Rank): 1 / rank of first relevant item clicked.
  • • Intra-List Diversity: Cosine distance between recommended items to avoid echo-chambers.
[PRODUCTION BUSINESS TELEMETRY]

Online Live Telemetry & A/B Metrics

  • • Take-Rate (Session CTR): Fraction of sessions ending in a play event.
  • • Median Time to Play: Seconds elapsed before user starts streaming.
  • • Day-28 Retention / Churn Rate: Ultimate business North-Star metric.
  • • Inverse Propensity Scoring (IPS): Counterfactual weighting to correct position bias.
